What Affects the Cost of Your Quote?

Every Huntsville estimate is tailored to your property rather than a one-size-fits-all guess. Here are the primary factors we evaluate when preparing your house cleaning quote:

  • Time Since Last Professional Clean: Homes that have not had baseboards, blinds, and bathroom tile hand-scrubbed in 6+ months require more detail labor than recently maintained homes.
  • Total Trim, Blinds & Bathroom Fixtures: Deep cleaning adds 10 vertical and high-reach detail tasks (baseboards, blinds, ceiling fans, door frames, heavy tub/tile buildup) across every active room.
  • Optional Interior Appliance Add-Ons: Adding inside-oven or inside-refrigerator scrubbing is priced transparently as a line-item option.

Step-by-Step Home Preparation Checklist

Follow these practical steps before your appointment so our specialists can focus 100% of their time on detailing your surfaces:

  • Clear Baseboard Perimeters & Window Sills: Moving small floor items, shoes, and decor slightly away from walls and window sills gives our team unobstructed access for hand-wiping baseboards and blinds.
  • Specify Optional Inside-Appliance Add-Ons: Standard 47-point deep cleans cover appliance exteriors; let us know when requesting your quote if you want inside-oven or inside-refrigerator detailing added.
  • Identify Delicate Stone or Specialty Finishes: Point out unsealed marble, butcher block, or specialty fixtures so our specialists apply the exact pH-neutral product required.
  • Confirm Entry & Parking Access: Our team arrives with full deep-detail supplies, extension dusters, and vacuums—clear driveway access speeds up setup.
