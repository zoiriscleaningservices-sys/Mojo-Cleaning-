How to Evaluate a Residential Cleaning Company

Whether you are hiring a weekly cleaner for your Huntsville residence or coordinating a lease turnover, use these five criteria to compare any cleaning provider.

Question 01

Do they publish an exact room-by-room checklist before you book?

Vague terms like "standard clean" or "deep clean" cause frustration when a cleaner skips baseboards, ceiling fans, or cabinet interiors.

Mojo Standard: We publish our complete 37-point Standard, 47-point Deep, and 56-point Move-In/Move-Out checklists openly online—plus explicit exclusions.

Question 02

Are the company and its technicians insured, bonded, and background-checked?

Uninsured independent cleaners leave homeowners financially exposed to accidental surface damage or on-site liability.

Mojo Standard: Mojo Cleaning LLC is fully insured and bonded, and every cleaning specialist is vetted and background-checked prior to dispatch.

Question 03

Do they bring their own professional supplies, HEPA vacuums, and microfiber cloths?

Using a homeowner’s worn mop or cross-contaminating cloths between bathrooms and kitchens spreads bacteria and scratches finishes.

Mojo Standard: We bring all supplies and equipment to every appointment, use clean microfiber cloths, and offer non-toxic, eco-friendly product options on request.

Question 04

What happens if a checklist item is accidentally missed?

Many providers offer no recourse once the payment is processed.

Mojo Standard: Every visit is protected by our 48-Hour Happiness Guarantee: notify us within 48 hours and our team returns to re-clean the spot free of charge.

Question 05

Can you customize your clean or skip unused rooms without rigid contracts?

Some national franchises lock clients into rigid rotational scripts or charge for guest bedrooms that are never used.

Mojo Standard: You can customize your checklist, skip unused rooms, or adjust your weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly schedule at any time.
