What Our Recurring House Cleaning Service Includes

Every Madison appointment follows our structured room checklist. You can also compare all three checklist tiers side by side on our Madison 56-point cleaning checklist page.

Scope Overview (37-Point Maintenance Checklist)

Recurring residential cleaning with Mojo Cleaning covers our 37-point room-by-room maintenance checklist across your kitchen (24 tasks), bathrooms (17 tasks), and bedrooms/living areas (15 tasks) on a weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly schedule. We bring all supplies and equipment, allow you to skip unused rooms, and back every visit with our 48-Hour Happiness Guarantee.

Kitchen (16 Recurring Tasks)

  • Sanitize and wipe all countertops and full backsplash area
  • Clean inside and outside of microwave oven
  • Degrease stove burners, stovetop, front panels, and control knobs
  • Clean and polish exterior of range hood, refrigerator, dishwasher, and oven
  • Scrub, sanitize, and dry kitchen sink, faucet, and soap dish
  • Wipe down kitchen table, chairs, barstools, and island surfaces
  • Dust overhead light fixtures, pendant globes, ceiling fans, and window sills
  • Empty trash and recycling bins, replace liners, and vacuum/mop all flooring

Bathrooms (12 Recurring Tasks)

  • Scrub and sanitize showers, tiled walls, glass enclosures, and bathtubs
  • Clean, disinfect, and polish toilets inside, outside, and around base
  • Sanitize vanity countertops, sinks, soap dishes, and polish chrome/brass fixtures
  • Clean and streak-free polish all vanity mirrors and glass surfaces
  • Dust towel bars, paper holders, light fixtures, and window sills
  • Empty bathroom wastebaskets, shake out bath mats, and scrub/mop floors

Bedrooms & Common Living Rooms (9 Recurring Tasks)

  • Remove cobwebs from corners, crown molding, and window frames
  • Dust all furniture surfaces, bookshelves, nightstands, and decor items
  • Dust ceiling fan blades, lampshades, and accessible light fixtures
  • Dust window sills, ledges, and tidy throw blankets/pillows
  • Make beds neatly (and change linens when fresh sheets are left on the bed)
  • Vacuum all carpets, area rugs, stairs, and mop hardwood/LVP/tile floors

What Affects the Cost of Your Quote?

Every Madison estimate is tailored to your property rather than a one-size-fits-all guess. Here are the primary factors we evaluate when preparing your house cleaning quote:

  • Visit Frequency (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly): Weekly and bi-weekly recurring schedules have lower soil buildup between visits and qualify for our best ongoing per-visit rates compared to monthly or one-time cleans.
  • Active Square Footage & Bathroom Count: Kitchens and bathrooms require the highest density of sanitization checkpoints (41 of our 56 total checklist points). Skipping unused bedrooms lowers active scope.
  • Pets & High-Traffic Flooring Types: Heavy pet hair or multi-level hardwood/tile staircases require additional HEPA vacuuming and damp mopping time.

Step-by-Step Home Preparation Checklist

Follow these practical steps before your appointment so our specialists can focus 100% of their time on detailing your surfaces:

  • Clear Everyday Clutter from Countertops & Floors: Picking up mail, toys, clothing, and dishes allows our specialists to spend 100% of their time sanitizing and polishing your actual surfaces.
  • Secure Pets or Share Pet Instructions: Let us know where friendly pets will be resting or if certain doors should remain closed while we bring equipment in and out.
  • Note Any Skipped Rooms or Priority Areas: If a guest bedroom or home office is closed off, tell us in advance so we can focus on high-traffic kitchens, baths, and family rooms.
  • Request Eco-Friendly Supplies if Preferred: We bring all standard professional supplies automatically; just let us know if you prefer our non-toxic, eco-friendly product option.
