What Our Move-In Cleaning Service Includes

Every Huntsville appointment follows our structured room checklist. You can also compare all three checklist tiers side by side on our Huntsville 56-point cleaning checklist page.

Scope Overview (56-Point Empty-Home Checklist)

Move-In Cleaning covers our complete 56-point empty-home checklist—combining all 47 Deep Cleaning tasks plus 9 interior storage tasks (sanitizing inside empty kitchen cabinets, drawers, pantry shelves, bathroom vanities, medicine cabinets, and bedroom closets) before your moving boxes arrive.

Kitchen Interior & Exterior Reset (24 Included Tasks)

  • Vacuum and wet-wipe the inside of all kitchen cabinets and drawers
  • Deep clean inside of oven, oven racks, and glass door
  • Clean and sanitize inside of refrigerator, freezer drawers, and door seals
  • Sweep pantry floor and wipe down all pantry shelving
  • Wet-wipe window blinds/shutters, baseboards, doors, frames, and all 20 Deep Clean tasks

Bathrooms Sanitization Reset (17 Included Tasks)

  • Clean and sanitize inside all bathroom vanity cabinets, drawers, and medicine cabinets
  • Descaling and disinfecting of showers, tubs, tile grout, toilets, and sinks
  • Wet-wipe bathroom window blinds/shutters, baseboards, doors, and frames
  • Polish mirrors, chrome/brass fixtures, exhaust vents, and scrub floors

Bedrooms, Closets & Living Areas (15 Included Tasks)

  • Wipe down all bedroom, hallway, and linen closet shelving and rods
  • Wet-wipe window blinds and shutters across all bedrooms and living areas
  • Hand-wipe baseboards, doors, door frames, window sills, and switch plates
  • Vacuum all carpet edges, stairs, and mop all hardwood, LVP, and tile flooring

Step-by-Step Home Preparation Checklist

Follow these practical steps before your appointment so our specialists can focus 100% of their time on detailing your surfaces:

  • Schedule After the Home Is Completely Empty: Book your Move-In Clean after the previous occupant’s belongings are out and before your movers unload furniture or kitchen boxes.
  • Verify Electricity, Running Water & Hot Water Are Active: Our team requires active lighting, electrical outlets for HEPA vacuums, and hot running water to sanitize kitchen and bathroom interiors thoroughly.
  • Schedule After Any Painting or Flooring Work Finishes: Ensure painters, floor installers, or handymen have completely finished and exited so new dust does not settle on freshly sanitized shelves.
  • Share Lockbox, Keypad, or Realtor Access Codes: You do not need to be present on-site—simply provide your lockbox or smart-lock code and we will text you when your new home is move-in ready.
